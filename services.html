Questions

Things people ask us

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free call we send a written quote with clear milestones, so you know the price before we start.

How long will it take?

A website usually takes a few weeks. Apps and custom software usually take two to five months. Each service above shows a typical range.

Can I use more than one service?

Yes, and many clients do. For example, design first, then development, then marketing. One team means nothing gets lost between steps.

Will I own the code and the designs?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the code and designs belong to you, and we hand over clear documentation.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work with clients around the world and plan our meetings around your time zone.

What happens after launch?

We stay closely involved right after launch to fix any issues quickly. After that you can pick a support plan or come back whenever you need something new.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what you are trying to do and we will point you the right way, even if that means we are not the right fit.
