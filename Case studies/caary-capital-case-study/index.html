The brief

Building a scalable finance operations workspace.

At Caary Capital, the mission was to eliminate corporate spending chaos for modern enterprises. The product required a high-density, rock-solid admin portal where finance managers could issue virtual cards, track multi-currency transactions, audit employee spending, and enforce limits in real time.

We took complete ownership of the frontend architecture from scratch — designing structured state flows with Redux Toolkit, crafting reusable form systems powered by React Hook Form & Zod, and optimizing data grids so dense ledger transactions render instantaneously without frame drops.

This case study uses recreated, portfolio-safe screens with placeholder data. No confidential operational or financial information is shown.

01 500+ Teams Supported

Scalable admin dashboards streamlined internal spending workflows and accelerated company onboarding.

02 Sub-Second Responsiveness

Code-splitting, route caching and memoized data tables kept heavy financial operations fast under stress.

03 60% Fewer Submission Errors

Strict schema validation, proactive inline error states and atomic field controls prevented erroneous transfers.
