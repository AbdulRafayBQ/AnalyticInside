The brief

Transforming clinical workflows into digital care.

Rheumatology Consultants approached us operating a clinic heavily reliant on telephone bookings, paper medical charts and disconnected retail counters. Patients living with chronic autoimmune and musculoskeletal conditions struggled with lengthy appointment delays and fragmented post-visit care instructions.

We engineered a tri-part ecosystem: a graceful React Native mobile app for patients, a secure Next.js clinic dashboard for practitioners, and an enterprise NestJS backend with PostgreSQL. The solution unifies appointment booking, clinical video libraries and specialized healthcare supplies into one harmonious experience.

This case study displays simulated clinic screens with test medical data. Strict HIPAA and health information privacy standards are maintained.

01 Unified Patient Hub

Blends clinic appointments, doctor guidance videos and health store purchases into one cohesive mobile experience.

02 Full-Stack Reliability

NestJS microservices and typed PostgreSQL schemas provide atomic scheduling and bulletproof inventory sync.

03 85% Faster Appointments

Automated slot reservation eliminated front-desk call bottlenecks and minimized in-clinic waiting room times.
