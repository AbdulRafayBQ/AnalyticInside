The brief

An internal tool, built for people who use it all day.

Salasa runs a logistics operation that lives or dies on fast, accurate decisions — which carrier gets an order, what a merchant gets charged, whether a shipment is on track. They needed an internal platform that could keep up with that pace. Our product team designed and built it end to end, turning a moving set of logistics requirements into one interface their operations staff could actually work in, every day.

The hard part was never the screens. It was making dense logistics logic — rule-based carrier assignation, capacity limits, service zones, merchant pricing — read clearly at a glance, without the product turning into a spreadsheet.

This case study uses recreated, portfolio-safe screens with placeholder data. No confidential operational information is shown.

How we built it

Product thinking, not just pixel delivery.

Consistency. Shared primitives cut cognitive load across every module in the product.

Resilience. Every screen accounts for loading, empty, error and partial-data states — not just the happy path.

Performance. Caching, targeted fetching and pagination keep dense workflows fast.

Maintainability. Typed interfaces and a feature-first structure make a complex domain easier to extend.
