The brief

An internal tool, built for people who use it all day.

Salasa runs a logistics operation that lives or dies on fast, accurate decisions — which carrier gets an order, what a merchant gets charged, whether a shipment is on track. They needed an internal platform that could keep up with that pace. Our product team designed and built it end to end.

The hard part was never the screens. It was making dense logistics logic — rule-based carrier assignation, capacity limits, service zones, merchant pricing — read clearly at a glance, without the product turning into a spreadsheet.

This case study uses portfolio-safe production screens and sanitized data. No confidential proprietary client information is displayed.

01Architecture that Scales

A maintainable frontend foundation — reusable components, predictable state and patterns the whole team can scale.

02Complexity Made Usable

Carrier logic, pricing matrices and capacity rules turned into interfaces operations staff can actually read.

03Operations at Speed

Sub-second search, fast bulk updates and clean keyboard interaction built for daily heavy duty usage.
