One name. Three businesses. One brand.

How Elyven gave Mateen Food Center a single look, a clear voice for each service and ads that reach people who actually order.

ClientMateen Food Center
Business linesBallroom · Catering · Restaurant
What we didBranding · Social · Reels · Ads
ChannelsFacebook · Instagram · WhatsApp
The challenge

Great food. No clear story online.

Mateen Food Center serves everything from biryani and karahi to burgers and rolls, and also runs a ballroom and a catering arm. Each customer wants something different, but online it all looked the same.

1

Three services, one confusing pageWedding hosts, party planners and diners all saw the same posts.

2

No consistent lookMenus, banners and posts were designed one at a time, with no system.

3

Ads reaching the wrong peopleBoosted posts went to a broad audience, so budget was spent on people who would never book or order.

What we built

A plan for every business line.

Each service got its own audience, creative and goal, all under one recognisable brand.

Filling the hall

Wedding and event bookings need trust and visuals. We showed the venue at its best and put it in front of people planning events.

  • Venue photo and reel shoots
  • Enquiry-form ads aimed at engaged and event-planning audiences
  • Wedding-season content calendar
ReelsLead adsPhotography

Winning big orders

Party, office and event catering is a high-value order. We made the menus and packages easy to understand and easy to ask about.

  • Menu and package creatives
  • Lead-form ads for party orders
  • WhatsApp quick replies and quote flow
Menu designWhatsAppLead forms

Dine-in and delivery

Daily footfall and online orders come from different moments. We ran dish-led reels and deals for both.

  • Dish and deal reels for dine-in
  • Local targeting around the outlet
  • Delivery order campaigns with retargeting
Dine-inDeliveryLocal ads
Strategy

Four moves behind the growth.

Brand

One look

A shared colour palette, type and template kit for posts, banners and menus.

Content

A steady plan

A monthly calendar with reels, dish shoots and deal posts, planned per service.

Ads

Tight targeting

Separate audiences for events, catering and diners, with exclusions to save budget.

Follow-up

Fast replies

Quick WhatsApp answers and retargeting so interested people do not go cold.
