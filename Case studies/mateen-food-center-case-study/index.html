The brief

Keeping three food and hospitality services recognizable under one brand.

Mateen Food Center serves customers across its ballroom, catering and dine-in/delivery offerings. Each has a different audience and reason to choose the brand, so its social presence needs to make every service easy to understand while still feeling unmistakably Mateen.

Elyven supports the brand with a consistent visual direction, social media post creative, short-form videos and advertising content. Campaign themes are shaped around the service being promoted—from event enquiries and catering to restaurant offers and delivery.

The aim is to turn attention into relevant enquiries, bookings and orders, then use campaign and sales data to understand what is contributing to business results. Revenue outcomes depend on the offer, audience, budget and sales follow-up.

01A Consistent Brand

Recognizable styling across posts and campaign creative helps customers connect different Mateen services to one name.

02Content for Each Audience

Ballroom, catering and restaurant promotions each get relevant messages, visuals and calls to action.

03Marketing with a Business Goal

Posts, videos and paid ads are planned to support discovery and encourage the next step: an enquiry, booking or order.

Make every campaign serve a clear business goal.

Stay recognizable. Use a consistent brand style across social posts, videos and advertising.

Speak to the right customer. Match each offer to the people most likely to need it.

Show the experience. Use food, venue and service-focused creative to make offers easy to understand.

Connect attention to action. Encourage the next step—an order, a catering enquiry or a ballroom booking—and review the available results.
