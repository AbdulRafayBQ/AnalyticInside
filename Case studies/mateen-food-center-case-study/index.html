The brief

Unifying a household culinary name without confusing its three distinct audiences.

Mateen Food Center had built decades of culinary loyalty in Karachi, but its three distinct business units — grand banquet ballrooms, corporate catering, and everyday dine-in/delivery — were colliding under one confusing brand umbrella.

Elyven rebuilt the entire brand architecture: establishing a shared luxury gold crest while deploying tailored customer funnels, dynamic WhatsApp quotes, and targeted video reels.

This case study uses portfolio-safe production screens and sanitized data. No confidential proprietary client information is displayed.

01Three Clear Funnels

Separate, dedicated lead-intake pathways for Ballroom tours, Corporate Catering proposals, and Delivery orders.

02Instant WhatsApp Pricing

Automated quick-reply matrices allowing prospective wedding clients to calculate estimated per-head cost.

03High-Converting Video Creative

Professional reel production highlighting authentic biryani craftsmanship, wedding hall aesthetics and hygienic kitchen standards.
