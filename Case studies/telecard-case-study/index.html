The brief

Consolidating decades of enterprise infrastructure into a fast modern web experience.

As one of Pakistan’s most established telecommunications and ICT infrastructure providers, Telecard had accumulated dozens of service offerings, technical whitepapers and client portals over decades. Their web presence was fragmented, difficult to navigate, and slow on mobile connections.

Elyven rebuilt the corporate site from the ground up: zero page-builder bloat, custom ACF Pro data modeling, semantic HTML5, and automated image compression achieving sub-2-second load times on regional 4G networks.

This case study uses portfolio-safe production screens and sanitized data. No confidential proprietary client information is displayed.

0194 Lighthouse Performance

Eliminated heavy third-party plugins in favor of lean custom theme code and optimized critical rendering path.

02Intuitive 2-Click Navigation

Restructured enterprise, cloud, and residential services so key solutions are accessible within two clicks.

03Empowered Marketing Team

Custom tailored CMS fields allow non-technical staff to publish service updates and press releases with zero code.
