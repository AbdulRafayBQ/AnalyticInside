01 / OVERVIEW

From blockchain complexity to a product people trust

A wallet experience engineered for absolute clarity.

Analytic Insider was brought in to design and build METADOT, a browser-extension wallet meant to make everyday crypto actions feel as simple as online banking — without hiding the control that Web3 users expect.

Our scope covered the full product surface: onboarding, wallet creation and import, multi-chain balance visibility, send and receive flows, network switching, custom RPC configuration, contact management and every feedback state in between — all inside the tight footprint of a browser extension.

The real challenge wasn't shipping screens. It was designing behaviour that could make high-stakes, irreversible actions — addresses, gas fees, network selection, transaction confirmation — feel understandable at a glance, for both first-time and advanced users.

A note on confidentiality The product shown here uses illustrative interface data. Real client branding, user data and financial details are withheld under NDA — this case study exists to walk through our process and design thinking.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Web3 is powerful. It rarely feels that way to use.

Before any interface work began, we mapped exactly where crypto-native products lose people's trust and attention.

Fragmented Multi-Chain Management

Users needed separate mental models for every network, with no single, trustworthy view of what they actually owned.

High-Stakes Actions, Low Confidence

Sending funds is irreversible. Most wallet UIs offered little reassurance before an action could not be undone.

Cluttered Competitor Patterns

Existing wallets buried core actions under dense settings and jargon, built for engineers rather than everyday users.

Security Without Simplicity Trade-offs

Seed phrases, validation and network config needed to stay rigorous, without turning every screen into a warning label.

03 / OUR APPROACH

A process built to de-risk complex product decisions.

Four structured phases, run by a small cross-functional pod, kept design, engineering and security decisions moving in the same direction.

PHASE 01

Discover & Audit

We audited existing wallet products, ran stakeholder interviews and mapped every blockchain action the extension would need to support — from balances to custom RPC.

PHASE 02

Architect the System

Before any screen was drawn, we defined the component and state architecture: how balances, networks and transaction states would behave consistently across the entire extension.

PHASE 03

Design the Interface

Every flow — onboarding, send, import, network switching — was prototyped, tested with real users and refined until the riskiest actions felt the clearest.

PHASE 04

Build, Test & Ship

Engineering built directly on the shared component system, with QA and security running validation on every state before the extension shipped to production.

04 / THE TEAM BEHIND IT

A focused pod, not a single point of failure.

METADOT was shaped by a small cross-functional team at Analytic Insider — strategy, design, engineering and security working in lockstep.

Product Strategist

Defined scope, success metrics and the roadmap linking user research to product decisions.

Lead Product Designer

Owned the interface system — onboarding, wallet flows and the interaction language across every screen.

Frontend Engineer, Web3

Built the extension's component architecture and connected every flow to on-chain state.

Blockchain Integration Engineer

Handled network configuration, RPC connections and transaction logic across six chains.

QA & Security Analyst

Stress-tested wallet flows, validation states and edge cases before every release.
