The brief

Demystifying irreversible Web3 transactions.

Web3 wallet extensions are notorious for intimidating user interfaces — confusing hex data strings, opaque gas limits, and catastrophic risks if an irreversible address typo occurs. METADOT was conceived as a non-custodial browser extension that makes everyday crypto operations as approachable as modern digital banking.

Analytic Insider took ownership of the complete product surface: initial seed-phrase onboarding, multi-chain balance aggregation, atomic token swaps, transaction simulation and custom RPC switching — all engineered to fit seamlessly inside the compact 360px popover footprint of a Chrome and Firefox extension.

This case study demonstrates public extension architecture and recreated test wallets. Private keys and sensitive blockchain telemetry are completely excluded.

01 Universal Multi-Chain

Native compatibility across Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Avalanche and Substrate ecosystems.

02 Human-Readable Signatures

EIP-712 formatted message previews decode obscure hex bytecode into transparent contract permissions.

Streamlined seed phrase generation and verification with instant social recovery backup options.
