The brief

A dual-ecosystem product for children's wellness.

Childhood nutritional tracking requires friction-free entry points. Caregivers and teachers don't have time to navigate multi-layered menus when logging hundreds of students. Morinaga required a synchronized ecosystem: an agile React Native mobile app for parents and staff, paired with an analytical Next.js dashboard for institutional reporting.

Our product team developed the entire mobile experience from scratch, optimizing form entry down to seconds, implementing WHO-standardized BMI growth curves, and engineering offline-first SQLite synchronization so attendance data never gets lost when network connectivity drops.

This case study uses recreated, portfolio-safe screens with anonymized data. No personal child health information is displayed.

01 Single Codebase Velocity

Shipped simultaneously on Android and iOS using React Native with 90%+ shared component architecture.

02 Offline-First Resilience

Local SQLite persistence with background sync ensures instant check-in even in areas without cellular coverage.

03 WHO Growth Benchmark

Automated percentile curves transform height and weight numbers into clear, actionable dietary guidance.
