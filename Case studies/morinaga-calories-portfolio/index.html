01 / Story

One product across mobile and web

A health platform built around fast, everyday data entry.

Our team designed and built Morinaga Calories as a cross-platform mobile app using React Native, giving parents and staff a consistent experience on both Android and iOS for everyday health and nutrition workflows.

The app lets caregivers manage child profiles, track BMI and calorie related information, log attendance, update account settings and keep everything synchronized. We paired it with a scalable Next.js dashboard for reporting, administration and product monitoring.

Throughout the build we focused on responsive UI, reusable components, dependable API communication and smooth interaction across device sizes, so profile and health data stays accurate wherever it's viewed.

Product Challenge

Keep the mobile experience simple for busy parents and staff, while giving the product enough structure underneath to support a connected web dashboard and a growing backend.
