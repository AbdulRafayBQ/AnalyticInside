The brief

Bringing heritage consumer goods into an interactive 3D digital flagship.

Dalda has been a trusted family kitchen staple for generations. While traditional e-commerce catalogs display flat, static product cutouts, Dalda Foods required an immersive digital shelf where consumers could spin, swipe and inspect bottle, pouch and tin formats with lifelike reflections.

Elyven designed a high-performance 3D packaging showcase: powered by Next.js 15 and Three.js WebGL, with GSAP scroll-bound physics and lightweight SVG fallback silhouettes ensuring zero layout shifts.

This case study uses portfolio-safe production screens and sanitized data. No confidential proprietary client information is displayed.

01Tactile 3D Shelf

Hardware-accelerated 3D pack rotation with touch inertia and pointer event tracking across all device form factors.

02Multi-Brand Catalog

Instant category switching between Dalda Premium Oils, Manpasand Banaspati, and Knockout Tea.

03Resilient Media Pipeline

Vector SVG fallback pack silhouettes ensure immediate layout stability even on slow regional networks.

What it does

Engineered for absolute reliability at scale.

Every component, query and user interaction was designed to balance visual polish with raw performance.

01
Next.js 15 & Three.js 3D Pipeline

Tactile pack interaction powered by Three.js WebGL rendering with GSAP hardware-accelerated inertia.

02
Multi-Brand Tab Matrix

Seamless switching between Dalda, Manpasand and Knockout lines without page refreshes.

03
Resilient Asset Loading

Instant SVG fallback silhouettes prevent any cumulative layout shift (CLS) during remote asset retrieval.

04
High-DPI Asset Rendering

Crisp nutritional labels and packaging typography razor-sharp on Retina and 4K mobile displays.

05
Zero Layout Shifts

Strict aspect-ratio locking maintaining smooth 60-FPS scrolling down the entire page.

06
Touch & Keyboard Navigation

Complete WCAG keyboard accessibility, screen-reader labels and natural swipe inertia.
