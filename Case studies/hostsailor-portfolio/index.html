01 / STORY

Modernizing a mature hosting platform

A complete frontend redesign built around clarity and accessibility.

I worked on a complete UI revamp of the HostSailor website, modernizing how customers discover hosting products, compare infrastructure options and move through high-intent conversion journeys.

The platform serves a broad hosting catalog—from VPS and dedicated servers to shared hosting, domains, SSL and infrastructure add-ons. That meant the redesign needed to simplify technical information without losing the detail developers and business customers need to make informed decisions.

I worked closely with the UI/UX designer and CTO throughout the redesign, aligning visual direction, technical feasibility, accessibility considerations and product priorities before translating the experience into frontend implementation.

CORE CHALLENGE

Transform a complex infrastructure website into a modern, accessible and conversion-friendly product experience while preserving the technical depth expected by hosting customers.

01 Complete UI modernization

Reworked visual hierarchy, sections, product presentation and page rhythm across the hosting experience.

02 Accessibility-led frontend

Optimized interaction and content presentation around WCAG accessibility standards and inclusive UX principles.

03 Cross-functional product delivery

Worked directly with the UI/UX designer and CTO to connect product design decisions with technical implementation.

03 / ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility treated as part of the product, not an afterthought.

The revamp was optimized around WCAG standards so navigation, content and interactive elements work more consistently for a wider range of users and input methods.

01Visual hierarchy & readability

Clear heading structure, spacing and content density help customers scan technical information more effectively.

02Contrast-conscious UI

Color and component states were considered with readability and accessible contrast in mind.

03Keyboard-friendly interaction

Interactive patterns were designed to remain understandable beyond pointer-only navigation.

04Visible interaction states

Buttons, links and controls use clearer feedback and focus treatment for better usability.

05Responsive accessibility

Content order and interface structure adapt cleanly across desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

06Semantic experience

Frontend structure was approached with meaningful content grouping and accessible page organization in mind.
