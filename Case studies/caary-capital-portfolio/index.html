01 / STORY

Building a scalable finance operations workspace

From product requirements to a complete internal FinTech platform.

At Caary Capital, I worked as the Lead Senior Frontend Software Engineer, taking ownership of the admin portal frontend from the ground up. The product was designed to help companies manage spending, transactions, business cards, user access, account activity and finance operations through one centralized interface.

My role went beyond implementation. I worked closely with QA engineers, backend developers, the Product Owner and Business Analysts to translate business requirements into reliable user flows, identify edge cases, define frontend behavior and move features from requirements through development, testing and production readiness.

The frontend needed to remain fast and maintainable while supporting dense financial data, complex tables, filters, account states, card controls, authentication flows and administrative configuration. I focused on reusable architecture, predictable state management, strong validation, performance optimization and consistent UX across the portal.
