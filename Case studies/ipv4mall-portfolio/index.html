The Brief

Making IPv4 brokerage easier to understand and act on.

I led the complete UI revamp of IPv4Mall, a global marketplace where organizations buy, sell and lease IPv4 address space. The platform serves customers across all five Regional Internet Registries and supports transaction journeys that involve technical verification, legal checks, escrow coordination and registry transfer.

The redesign focused on making those journeys legible. I worked closely with the UI/UX designer and CTO to improve information hierarchy, service discovery, trust messaging, calls to action, responsive behavior and overall frontend consistency across the product.

Accessibility was treated as a core part of the build from day one. I shaped the interface around WCAG standards, paying attention to readable content structure, contrast that stays legible, clear interaction states, smooth keyboard navigation and predictable responsive layouts.

Product Challenge

Turn a highly technical brokerage process that depends on trust into a clear, accessible experience for buyers, sellers and organizations operating across multiple global registries.
