The brief

Demystifying multi-million dollar internet commodity trades.

IPv4 addresses are an exhausted global resource trading at premium rates among telecommunications giants, cloud providers and hosting conglomerates. Historically, transacting IP subnets involved opaque broker negotiations, lengthy registry approval back-and-forths, and substantial escrow risks.

IPv4Mall required a modern institutional platform that brings financial-grade clarity to IP acquisitions. We engineered an accessible frontend equipped with dynamic CIDR calculators, real-time blacklist checks, automated transfer milestone tracking, and seamless buyer-seller matching across all five Regional Internet Registries.

This case study demonstrates platform UX architecture and public marketplace data flows. Proprietary client escrow contracts and private subnet ranges remain confidential.
