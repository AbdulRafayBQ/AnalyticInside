01 / STORY

One platform for the patient journey

Making healthcare easier to discover, access and manage.

I developed the DocLink healthcare website for a Pakistan-based startup, starting from wireframes and building the frontend experience from scratch. The goal was to create a unified patient journey where people could access doctors online, book in-clinic visits, explore specialists, take lab tests and use prescribed healthcare packages.

Beyond the public website, the product required a scalable dashboard architecture capable of supporting both web and mobile experiences. The interface needed to balance healthcare trust, large amounts of medical information, conversion-focused booking flows and responsive behavior across devices.

PRODUCT CHALLENGE

Turn a broad healthcare ecosystem—doctors, specialties, diseases, consultations, subscriptions, labs and reports—into a simple experience that patients can understand quickly.
