The brief

Making healthcare accessible, transparent and reliable.

DocLink was built for a high-growth health-tech initiative in Pakistan aimed at solving fragmented patient experiences. Traditional healthcare discovery involved cumbersome phone lines, unverified credentials, long clinic waits and disconnected diagnostic records.

We engineered the frontend ecosystem from wireframes to high-performance production screens — establishing a robust design architecture with React, Next.js and Redux Toolkit that handles heavy directory listings, doctor schedules, interactive clinic calendars and responsive mobile viewports seamlessly.

This case study uses portfolio-safe screenshots with simulated consultation data. Confidential patient health records are strictly protected.

01 Complete Patient Lifecycle

Integrated provider discovery, real-time schedule selection, tele-consultation and prescriptions in one journey.

02 Ultra-Low Latency Booking

Server-side rendering with Next.js and optimistic state updates halved the average appointment booking time.

03 Universal Accessibility

Mobile-first responsive architecture designed for diverse devices, weak 3G connections and broad patient demographics.
