02 Our story

It started with one stubborn question.

Why does so much software look wonderful in a demo and fall apart in real life? In 2018 a few of us started answering that for local businesses. No office, no pitch deck, just working builds that people could click on.

Those builds became products, the products became friendships with clients, and the friendships became a studio. Our standard has not changed since day one: if it does not work in someone's hands, it is not finished.

Browse our portfolio
2018

The first build

Four of us, one shared table and a booking system for a local clinic. It is still running today, which we are quietly proud of.

2020

Freelancers no more

We set up proper design and engineering teams, signed our first client outside the United States and started owning products from the first sketch to the last release.

2022

Products and platforms

Fintech dashboards, healthcare platforms and mobile apps began serving thousands of people every day. We learned a lot about what breaks when real traffic arrives.

2024

AI, used sensibly

We added automation and assistants to what we offer, but only where they save real time. If a simple form does the job, we will tell you to keep the form.

2026

Where we are today

Strategy, design, engineering, AI and brand under one roof in Dearborn, with clients on four continents and a team that still answers its own emails.
