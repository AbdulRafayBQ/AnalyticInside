OUR STORY

It started with one
stubborn question.

Why does so much software look impressive in a demo and fall apart in real use? In 2018 a small team of engineers and designers started answering that question for a handful of local businesses — no office, no pitch deck, just working builds.

Those builds turned into products, those products turned into partnerships, and the partnerships turned into a studio. Today we ship platforms, mobile apps and AI systems for founders, clinics, fintechs and enterprises across four continents — but the standard hasn't moved: if it doesn't work in someone's hands, it isn't done.
